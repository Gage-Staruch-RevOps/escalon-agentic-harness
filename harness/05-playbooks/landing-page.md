# Landing page

1. **Brief** (5 minutes): who it's for, the one action, the offer, proof, voice, and the brand guide to follow.
2. **Copy before pixels**: `copywriting` for structure (headline, problem, offer, proof, CTA). One CTA.
3. **Design**: the brand guide first, `frontend-design` for a distinctive layout, `apple-design` for motion. Phone width first.
4. **Build**: semantic HTML, real form handling into a tool the company approves.
5. **Review**: `web-design-guidelines` pass, `web-quality-audit` (performance, accessibility, SEO), `break-ui` with worst-case content.
6. **Preview** wherever the team hosts previews. Publishing to the public site needs a yes.
7. **Report**: preview link, screenshots at phone and desktop width, what needs sign-off.

## Done means

Preview link, checked at phone width, audit clean, copy free of em dashes, sign-off list ready.
