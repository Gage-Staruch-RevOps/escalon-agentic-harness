# iOS app

1. Design intent first: `apple-design` (fluid motion, materials, typography) plus the team's brand where it applies.
2. Architecture and views: `swiftui-expert-skill`, `swiftui-pro`; modern APIs and Liquid Glass: `swiftui-liquid-glass`.
3. Language: `write-swift` (Swift 6 concurrency, value types).
4. Run and check in the simulator (`ios-simulator-skill`, or the iOS Simulator tool), screenshots of every state.
5. Signing and release go through the team's Apple developer account and process. EDIT: bundle id prefix, team, who uploads builds.

## Done means

Builds clean, runs in the simulator, screenshots of main states, no concurrency warnings.
