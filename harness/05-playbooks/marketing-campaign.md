# Marketing campaign

1. **Goal and offer**: one measurable goal, one offer, one audience.
2. **Plan**: `campaign-plan` (channels, calendar, metrics).
3. **Copy**: `copywriting` in the company's voice. Problem in the reader's words, specific.
4. **Emails**: `email-sequence`; **social**: `social`; **page**: the `landing-page` playbook.
5. **Tracking**: `analytics` (UTMs, conversion events) so results land somewhere real.
6. Drafts go to the user; nothing is sent or posted without their yes.

## Done means

A calendar, every asset drafted in voice, tracking defined, nothing sent without approval.
